Map View

Switch between the whole world and a close-up of your area, or alternate automatically. The local view uses sharper NASA imagery for the day/night maps and adds coastlines, state lines and towns (Local Detail layer). Local layers — aircraft, ships, light pollution, radar, fires, satellite clouds — fetch more detail when zoomed in. Each layer in Map Layers can also be set to show in both views, only the world view or only the local view, so the auto-switch can bring in local layers (river gauges, storm reports, rain totals) when it zooms in and drop them when it zooms out. Changes apply immediately.

NORAD numbers, comma-separated (up to 6). Blank = ISS (25544), Tiangong (48274) and Hubble (20580).

Home Assistant Alerts

Pi-Clock can tell Home Assistant when a bright ISS / Tiangong / Hubble pass is about to start or the aurora may be visible from home, so an automation can notify your phone, flash a light or speak on a speaker. In Home Assistant, create an automation with a Webhook trigger and paste its URL here (e.g. http://192.168.1.90:8123/api/webhook/your-id).

Stored only on this Pi (root-only file). Use the IP address rather than homeassistant.local.
Which satellites: see "Satellites for Visible Passes" in the Passes & Tides card.
Coast Panel

Latest wave height, wave period, water temperature and wind from NOAA buoys (enable the Coast panel in the Applets card).

NDBC station IDs, up to 4. Blank = Wrightsville Beach (41110), Diamond Shoals (41025), Frying Pan Shoals (41013).
Tonight's Sky (Astronomy)

Sun, moon and darkness times are calculated on the Pi. With an Astrospheric Pro API key the panel also shows hourly cloud, transparency (including smoke) and seeing for your QTH, and picks the best clear, dark window. Enable the panel in the Applets card above.

Stored only on this Pi (root-only file). Leave blank and save to keep the current key.
Pro includes 100,000 API credits per month; each refresh uses about 65.

Volcanoes

Volcanoes in the Smithsonian / USGS Weekly Volcanic Activity Report. Red with a plume = new activity or unrest; orange = continuing eruption.

Hide volcanoes with long-running, continuing eruptions
Hurricanes / Tropical Storms

Active tropical cyclones worldwide from GDACS. Atlantic and eastern Pacific storms use the National Hurricane Center forecast. Updated every 30 minutes.

NWS Weather Alerts

Active US National Weather Service alerts. Storm-based warnings (tornado, severe thunderstorm, flash flood) are outlined on the map; alerts covering your QTH appear as a banner at the top of the screen. Updated every 3 minutes.

Map shows severe and extreme storm-based warnings
Live Radar

US NEXRAD base reflectivity mosaic from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, refreshed every 5 minutes. Covers the contiguous US. Zoom the map toward your QTH for more detail; the image resolution follows the zoom.

Air Quality

US Air Quality Index around your QTH from the Open-Meteo Air Quality API (CAMS model data), updated every 90 minutes. Clean air is left transparent; moderate or worse air (wildfire smoke, ozone) is shaded in EPA colours.

Starlink Trains

Recently launched Starlink batches (CelesTrak data), drawn as a string of dots with the next pass visible from your QTH: train above 10°, sunlit, and your sky dark. Trains spread out and fade after a week or two.

Aircraft (ADS-B)

Live aircraft around your QTH from the adsb.lol community network (free), updated every 30 seconds and moved smoothly in between. Colour shows altitude (green low → violet high); amber outline = military; red ring = emergency squawk.

If you run readsb, dump1090 or tar1090 on your network, enter its host:port to use your own receiver instead of adsb.lol.
Severe Storm Outlook (SPC)

NOAA Storm Prediction Center convective outlook in SPC colours: general thunder (light green) through marginal, slight, enhanced, moderate and high risk. A note at the bottom-left names the risk at your QTH. US only; updated through the day.

Wildfires & Smoke

NASA FIRMS satellite fire detections from the last 24 hours (US, plus nearby Canada and Mexico), sized by fire intensity, with NOAA HMS analyst-drawn smoke plumes (light, medium, heavy).

Satellite Clouds

Real infrared satellite imagery from GOES-East, GOES-West and Himawari via NASA GIBS (about an hour behind real time), shown as clouds over the map. Covers the Americas, Pacific, Asia and Australia; Europe, Africa and the Indian Ocean have no free geostationary feed here. Zooming in fetches sharper imagery.

Rocket Launches

Upcoming launches worldwide from The Space Devs Launch Library. Pads near your QTH (Cape Canaveral and Wallops from the Carolinas) glow gold — those launches are often visible from home. Markers appear on the map only for launches coming up soon; the Rocket Launches panel (Applets card) still lists the next several.

Planets

Marks the point on Earth where each naked-eye planet is directly overhead right now. For when and where to look from home, enable the Planets & Sky Events panel in the Applets card.

Ships (AIS)

Live vessel positions from the aisstream.io network: arrows for ships under way, dots for anchored or moored. Covers the visible map when zoomed in, or about 1,000 km around your QTH at full-world view. Needs a free aisstream.io API key.

Stored only on this Pi (root-only file).
Aurora Watch

Draws NOAA SWPC's aurora view line (solid red: aurora may be seen low on the northern horizon from here northward) and a dotted photo line further south, from the live OVATION model. A banner appears when your QTH is north of either line at night, or when the 3-day Kp forecast says it will be. Cloud cover comes from your Tonight's Sky (Astrospheric) panel when it's enabled — no extra API credits. Enable the Aurora Watch panel in the Applets card for the Kp chart.

Citizen-science reports show cameras often catch aurora several degrees south of SWPC's line. This also sets the Kp your home needs for a "photo" alert.
Light Pollution

Estimated night-sky brightness, computed on the Pi from NASA's VIIRS night-lights composite with a skyglow model, coloured by approximate Bortle class (blue/green darkest → yellow, orange, red, white brightest). A note at the bottom gives the estimate at home and the nearest Bortle 3 sky. It's an estimate: terrain, trees and changes since 2016 aren't modelled.

Storm Reports

Tornado, hail, wind-damage, flood and snow/ice reports from the National Weather Service (via Iowa Environmental Mesonet), fading with age. Labelled with type and size in the local view.

Rain Totals

How much rain actually fell (NOAA MRMS radar estimates corrected with rain gauges), contiguous US.

River & Creek Gauges

NOAA river gauges near home, coloured by flood status: blue normal, yellow action stage, orange minor, red moderate, purple major flooding. A ring means the forecast reaches flood stage. Zoom in for names and levels.

Rare Birds (eBird)

Recent sightings eBird flags as rare or unusual for the area and season, near home. Zoom in to see the species at each spot. Needs a free eBird API key.

Stored only on this Pi (root-only file).
Dark-Sky Sites Tonight

Nearby observing sites, coloured by tonight’s cloud cover during astronomical darkness (green clear, yellow partly cloudy, grey cloudy), with estimated Bortle class. A note names the best site tonight.

Blank = Jordan Lake, Medoc Mountain SP, Kerr Lake, Uwharrie NF, Staunton River SP (VA), Pettigrew SP and Lake Mattamuskeet. Up to 10, e.g. Backyard:35.69:-78.71;Jordan Lake:35.736:-79.017
Visible Pass Tracks

Draws where each visible ISS / Tiangong / Hubble pass travels across the map (best in the local view), with the start time and peak height. Satellites are set in the Passes & Tides card.
